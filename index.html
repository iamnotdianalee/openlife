Life Participant AgreementVersion 14 · 31 pages · Full text below

1. Definitions

1.1 “Participant” means the person who taps Accept. 1.2 “Stream” means any data captured under the Participant’s plan, including data from the ring, the OPENLIFE app and any Home Unit. 1.3 “Marketplace” means the platform on which OPENLIFE offers Streams to Buyers.

2. Plans

2.1 The Participant selects one plan: Basic, Full or Total. 2.2 A move to a higher plan takes effect immediately. A move to a lower plan requires written notice and the approval of the Care Team. 2.3 OPENLIFE may extend the content of a plan by notice in the app.

3. Devices and permissions

3.1 The Participant wears the OPENLIFE ring. 3.2 The OPENLIFE app may request access to location and screen time and, on Full and Total, to messages, calls, searches and reading history. Each “Allow” is a separate consent. 3.3 A Home Unit is installed in each room of the Participant’s residence. Microphones are active on Full and Total. Cameras are active on Total.

4. Ownership

4.1 Under the Data Ownership Act 2035 the Participant licenses all Streams to OPENLIFE, exclusively and worldwide, for the duration of this Agreement and after it. 4.2 Consent under the Act is recorded when the Participant taps Accept.

5. Third parties

5.1–5.3 Reserved. 5.4 Where a third party appears in a Stream and no consent is on file, OPENLIFE masks the party’s identity. What was said, written and heard remains part of the Stream.

6. Marketplace and certification

6.1 Streams may be offered to Buyers, including AI developers, insurers, employers and researchers. 6.2 Only moments verified as genuine by an independent audit are certified as Certified Human and paid in full. The audit decides. 6.3 OPENLIFE sets the price of each sale.

7. Private time

7.1 The ring may be removed. 7.2 Removal must be confirmed by the Participant. 7.3 Any period in which the ring is not worn is recorded as Private time and charged at €10 per hour.

8. Term

8.1 This Agreement runs until ended. 8.2 The licence in clause 4.1 survives termination. 8.3 This Agreement is governed by the Data Ownership Act 2035 as interpreted by OPENLIFE Legal.

STATE

Accordingly, the parties agree to enter into this employment contract, which shall be governed by the following

CLAUSES

FIRST.– PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS
1.1The WORKER is hired as a Data Worker to provide the COMPANY with her Recorded Life, understood as the set of physiological, behavioural, audiovisual, emotional and location data generated by the WORKER during the term of this contract (hereinafter, the “Data”).
1.2The COMPANY may verify, label, package and transfer the Data to third parties as “Certified Human” data.
1.3“Marketplace” means the platform on which the COMPANY offers the Data to Buyers.
SECOND.– PROFESSIONAL CATEGORY AND LEVEL
2.1The WORKER is classified in contribution group 10, professional category Data Worker – Level 3 (Total), as described in Annex I.
2.2A move to a higher Level takes effect immediately. A move to a lower Level requires fifteen (15) days’ written notice and the approval of the Care Team, and shall not entitle the WORKER to the deletion of Data already recorded.
2.3The COMPANY may extend the content of a Level by notice in the OPENLIFE application.
THIRD.– WORKING TIME, DEVICES AND PERMISSIONS
3.1Due to the nature of the services, working time is continuous: twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, including periods of rest, sleep, illness, holidays and leave of any kind. Rest periods do not interrupt the provision of services.
3.2The COMPANY shall provide the WORKER with: (a) a recording ring, model OL-R3; (b) a Home Unit, model HU-1, to be installed in each room of the WORKER’s home; and (c) the OPENLIFE application.
3.3The devices remain the property of the COMPANY. The WORKER shall pay a monthly rental fee of forty euros (€40.00), which shall be deducted from the salary.
3.4The WORKER shall wear the recording ring at all times. The removal of the ring is regulated exclusively by the Seventh Clause.
3.5The OPENLIFE application may request access to location and screen time and, on Levels 2 and 3, to messages, calls, searches and reading history. Each “Allow” is a separate consent. The microphones of the Home Unit are active on Levels 2 and 3. Its cameras are active on Level 3.
FOURTH.– OWNERSHIP AND TRANSFER OF RIGHTS
4.1In accordance with the Data Ownership Act, the WORKER transfers to the COMPANY all rights over the Data on an exclusive, irrevocable and perpetual basis, for the whole world.
4.2Consent under the Data Ownership Act is recorded when the WORKER signs this contract.
4.3The COMPANY may use the Data for any purpose, including the training of artificial intelligence systems, and may transfer it to third parties without informing the WORKER.
4.4The WORKER expressly waives the right to erasure of the Data. The Data shall not be deleted after the termination of this contract.
FIFTH.– THIRD PARTIES
5.1The WORKER acknowledges that persons who live with her or come into contact with her, including partners, children, relatives, friends and guests (hereinafter, “Third Parties”), will be recorded incidentally.
5.2The WORKER undertakes to inform Third Parties by placing the official OPENLIFE sticker at the entrance of her home. The COMPANY shall not be liable for any failure to inform Third Parties.
5.3Holders of an OPENLIFE Invisible membership shall be fully removed from the Data.
5.4Where a Third Party appears in the Data and no consent is on file, the COMPANY masks the identity of that Third Party. What was said, written and heard remains part of the Data.
SIXTH.– MARKETPLACE, CERTIFICATION AND HIGH-VALUE EVENTS
6.1The Data may be offered on the Marketplace to Buyers, including AI developers, insurers, employers, studios and researchers.
6.2Only moments verified as genuine by an independent audit are certified as “Certified Human” and paid in full. The audit decides.
6.3The COMPANY sets the price of each sale.
6.4The following shall be considered High-Value Events: (a) illness or injury; (b) domestic or family conflict; (c) separation or divorce; (d) bereavement; (e) pregnancy and childbirth; (f) accidents; (g) any other event that the COMPANY classifies as such, according to market demand.
6.5Data recorded during a High-Value Event of a WORKER of Level 3 shall be remunerated as a supplement at three (3) times the ordinary rate (Annex I, line 5).
6.6The COMPANY may ask the WORKER to confirm or describe a High-Value Event through the OPENLIFE application. Failure to respond within twenty-four (24) hours shall be deemed confirmation.
6.7The deliberate provocation, simulation or staging of High-Value Events constitutes a very serious offence and grounds for disciplinary dismissal.
SEVENTH.– PRIVATE TIME (UNRECORDED TIME)
7.1The recording ring may be removed.
7.2The removal must be confirmed by the WORKER in the OPENLIFE application.
7.3Any period during which the ring is not worn, or any device is covered, obstructed or switched off, shall be considered Unrecorded Time (also called “Private time”). Each full hour of Unrecorded Time shall be deducted from the salary at a rate of ten euros (€10.00).
7.4More than twenty (20) hours of Unrecorded Time in one calendar month may result in a change to a lower Level, at the sole discretion of the COMPANY.
7.5No exceptions shall apply for reasons of a personal, family or emotional nature.
EIGHTH.– REMUNERATION
8.1The WORKER shall receive the gross monthly salary of her Level, as stated in Annex I. For Level 3 (Total) it is two thousand euros (€2,000.00), distributed as follows: (a) one thousand six hundred euros (€1,600.00) in cash, by bank transfer; and (b) four hundred euros (€400.00) in kind, in the form of app access credits.
8.2The salary shall be paid in twelve (12) monthly instalments on the last working day of each month. No extraordinary payments shall be made.
8.3The share of the Marketplace revenue due to the WORKER is stated in Annex I, line 4. It is paid with the supplement for the moment sold.
NINTH.– WELLBEING PROGRAMME
9.1The WORKER shall have free access to the OPENLIFE Wellbeing Programme (psychological support, meditation and support groups).
9.2Wellbeing sessions shall be recorded and shall count as working time.
TENTH.– DURATION AND PROBATIONARY PERIOD
10.1This contract shall take effect on 3 March 2053 for a period of twelve (12) months and shall be tacitly renewed for successive periods of equal duration unless either party gives notice.
10.2A probationary period of one (1) month is established.
ELEVENTH.– TERMINATION
11.1The WORKER may terminate this contract with three (3) months’ notice. Termination without notice shall oblige the WORKER to pay the COMPANY an amount equal to three (3) monthly salaries.
11.2After termination, the WORKER may not enter into a new contract with the COMPANY for a period of twenty-four (24) months.
TWELFTH.– PERSONAL DATA AND GOVERNING LAW
12.1The COMPANY shall process the WORKER’s identification data for the sole purpose of managing this employment relationship, in accordance with applicable data protection legislation. This clause does not apply to the Data, which is the property of the COMPANY under the Fourth Clause.
12.2This contract shall be governed by Spanish law and by the Data Ownership Act. For any dispute, the parties submit to the Labour Courts of Barcelona.
